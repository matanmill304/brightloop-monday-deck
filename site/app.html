For reviewers · what this is

Built for Maya, Head of Growth. Every Monday she spent 2 hours screenshotting Lovable's, Replit's and Bolt's pricing pages and homepages into Slides for the 11:00 leadership meeting. This builds that deck, from the competitors' own pages only.

Prove it

./run --input recordings/weeks/2026-W43/cases.json --output result.jsonfresh clone, zero keys, no install: our recorded week
./run --input cases.json --output result.jsonyour file, or upload it below: the same pipeline

How it decides · the first rule that applies wins

  1. Unsafe or unusable (instructions aimed at AI, a name collision, no text) → skipped
  2. Outside the week → skipped
  3. Not from the competitor (press, Reddit, ads, rumors) → "verify on the official page"
  4. A change on its own pricing page or homepage → item
  5. Its own announcement elsewhere → item, "check the page"; features go to Noa, ads to Daniel
